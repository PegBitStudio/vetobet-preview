How it works

Three steps. One honest number.

Every sportsbook price hides a fee. Take the fee out and compare books, and the good prices stand out.

1

Compare licensed sportsbooks

We collect the price of the same bet from FanDuel, DraftKings, BetMGM, Caesars, BetRivers and more.

2

Remove the cut

Each book adds its fee to every price. We strip it out and average the books to get a fair price for the bet.

3

See the gap

When a book pays more than the fair price, you see it in green. When it pays less, you see that in red.

Proof, not promises

A record nobody can quietly edit.

Every price gap we show is saved before kickoff, each entry locked to the one before it. After the game we grade it against the market's closing price. Wins and misses both stay. This is the part no one can copy by writing code, because it is made of time.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Is VetoBet a sportsbook?

No. VetoBet never takes bets or holds money. It compares prices from licensed sportsbooks so you can decide where, and whether, to bet.

What is a "fair price"?

Every sportsbook adds a fee to its prices. We remove each book's fee and average the books, which gives the price a bet would have with no fee. If a book pays more than that, the bet is priced in your favour.

Will this make me win?

No tool can promise that. Taking better-than-fair prices improves your results over many bets, but any single bet can still lose. Bet only what you can afford to lose.

How often are prices updated?

At least once a day, and the time of the last check is always shown next to the prices.

Which sportsbooks do you compare?

Licensed US books such as FanDuel, DraftKings, BetMGM, Caesars and BetRivers. Other books help us work out the fair price, but we only ever point you to licensed ones.

Can I cancel a paid plan?

Yes, at any time, from Plan & billing in the app.
