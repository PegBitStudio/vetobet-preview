Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

In short: we collect only what the service needs, we never sell your data, and we never see your card number. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your email, an optional display name, and your password. Passwords are never stored as text; we keep only a scrambled (hashed) version.
  • What you give the tools: bets you log, slips you paste or upload (including screenshots), questions you ask Veto AI, and picks you stamp.
  • Plan and billing status: which plan you are on. Card details go straight to Stripe and never reach us.
  • Basic technical data: your network address and browser details, used to keep the service secure, limit abuse and count daily free slip checks.
  • Cookies and local storage: a sign-in cookie, an access cookie if the site is behind a passcode, and your light or dark theme choice. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

2. Why we use it

  • To run your account and the tools you use.
  • To send you account emails, such as password recovery codes.
  • To keep the service safe and fair, and to find and fix problems.
  • To process payments and manage your plan.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it to target ads.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect people's safety or our rights.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account and bets while your account is open. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your personal data, except records we must keep by law (such as payment records) and anonymous totals that no longer identify you. Entries in the public record are about picks, not people.

5. Your choices

  • Ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you.
  • Sign out at any time, and clear cookies in your browser.
  • Ask us to stop sending non-essential emails (we send only account emails).

Depending on where you live, you may have extra rights (for example in California). To use any of these, contact us at __CONTACT__ and we will reply as soon as we can.

6. Security

We protect data with encrypted connections, hashed passwords, access limits and regular testing. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

7. Age

VetoBet is for people aged 21 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you think a younger person has given us data, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we will tell you in the app or by email. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
