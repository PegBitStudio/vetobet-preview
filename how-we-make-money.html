How VetoBet makes money

You should know who pays for the tool you use to judge prices. Here it is, plainly.

The rule: the "best price" we show is always the best price from the licensed sportsbooks we compare. Money never changes it, and no sportsbook can pay to be ranked higher.

Where the money comes from

SourceHow it works
SubscriptionsThe free plan stays free. Paid plans (Pro and Edge) unlock unlimited slip checks and extra tools. This is our main business.
Sportsbook partnersWhen you sign up to a sportsbook through some of our links, that sportsbook may pay us a fee. It costs you nothing and does not change your offer. Links that earn us money are labelled.
Data and API (planned)Developers and media sites will be able to pay for access to our prices and our public record.

What we promise

  • We never take bets, hold your money, or earn when you lose.
  • We show gaps in both directions. A bad price is shown in red even if the sportsbook is a partner.
  • We never sell your personal data. See our Privacy Policy.
  • Example or experimental screens say so. No made-up reviews, wins or user counts.
  • Our public record is saved before games start and cannot be edited afterwards.
